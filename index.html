Research

Undergraduate thesis, July 2026 – ongoing

Patient-specific CFD-based hemodynamic analysis and machine learning prediction of coronary artery flow patterns associated with plaque formation and rupture risk

  • Investigating hemodynamic markers linked to plaque formation and rupture, to support patient-specific cardiovascular risk assessment.
  • Running patient-specific transient CFD simulations on 3D-reconstructed coronary CTA geometries to characterize wall shear stress (WSS), with OSI and RRT analysis planned.
  • Segmenting medical images and reconstructing coronary geometry in Materialise Mimics and 3-matic, then simulating in ANSYS Fluent.
  • Generating flow-sensitive geometry variations with MorphMan to augment datasets for machine learning.
  • Developing a deep learning surrogate to predict coronary hemodynamic fields and derived risk parameters efficiently.
